START WITH THE BASICS

Forex, in a nutshell.

Forex means foreign exchange. Traders compare one currency with another. In EUR/USD, EUR is the base currency and USD is the quote currency.

01

Choose a pair

For example, EUR/USD compares the euro with the US dollar.

02

Read the quote

A sample quote of 1.0842 means one euro is worth 1.0842 US dollars.

03

Manage your risk

Prices can move either way. Learn first and never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
